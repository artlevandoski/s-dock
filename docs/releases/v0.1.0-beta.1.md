# S-Dock 0.1.0 beta 1

The first public beta of **S-Dock**, a controller-friendly Windows frontend for certain game
libraries. Point it at your own game folders and your own emulator, and browse everything in a
full-screen carousel or a grid, with covers, backgrounds, logos and descriptions filled in for you.

> S-Dock does not include or download games, firmware, keys or system files, and it never changes
> your game files. You bring your own games and emulator.

## Download

**`S-Dock-0.1.0-beta.1-win-x64.zip`**: unzip it and you have an **S-Dock** folder with everything in
it. There's nothing to install.

The other files (`.nupkg`, `releases.win.json`, `assets.win.json`, `RELEASES`) are for future
in-place updates; you don't need to download them.

**Requirements:** 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Nothing else needs installing.

## Getting started

1. Unzip the download wherever you like: Documents, another drive or a USB stick (not
   `C:\Program Files`, where Windows doesn't let programs save).
2. Run `S-Dock.exe` in the S-Dock folder. This beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen
   may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Open **Settings** (the gear, or Select on a controller):
   - **Emulator Settings…** to add your emulator (put emulator builds in the S-Dock folder's
     `emulators` folder and they're found automatically);
   - **Add Folder…** to add your games;
   - let S-Dock download **TitleDB** when it asks, to fill in names, descriptions and pictures.

Everything S-Dock saves (library, artwork, TitleDB, settings) stays in the `data` folder inside
the S-Dock folder, and nowhere else on your PC. To move S-Dock, move the folder; to remove it,
delete the folder.

## What's in this beta

**Browse**
- Full-screen carousel with square covers, the game's logo, a one-line description and Play; or
  a grid with a details panel.
- Separate *Main Library* and *Arcade Archives* tabs, with optional ACA NEOGEO and SEGA AGES.
- Jump by letter, filters (including *Update available*), search, sort titles and hidden games.
- Backgrounds from the game's artwork, a gradient in the game's colour, or plain.

**Play**
- Emulator profiles, a default per console generation, and a per-game choice.
- PC versions (recompilations and ports that are their own `.exe`) run without an emulator.
- Esc or Start + Select closes the game and brings you back, optionally with a second press to
  confirm.

**Controller first**
- Xbox-compatible controllers work everywhere: browsing, the on-screen Settings and Game Actions
  menus, and every dialog. S-Dock comes back to the front on its own after a game.

**Game info and artwork**
- [TitleDB](https://github.com/blawar/titledb) fills in names, descriptions, square icons, banners
  and screenshots, matched exactly by each file's title ID (using the US, UK, Australian and
  Japanese listings).
- [nx-versions](https://github.com/16BitWonder/nx-versions) shows whether the update in your game
  folder is the latest.
- Optional [IGDB](https://www.igdb.com/) and [SteamGridDB](https://www.steamgriddb.com/) for logos,
  extra artwork and games TitleDB doesn't list (free accounts; your keys stay on your PC).
- Choose any cover, background or logo, or use your own image.

**Self-contained**
- Everything lives in the S-Dock folder, so it can move between PCs or run from a USB drive.

## Known limitations

- Game Actions are available in the grid only; the carousel is kept to browsing and playing.
- Typing (search, text boxes) and Windows' file and folder pickers still need a keyboard and mouse.
- Your existing library isn't brought across automatically: copy the `data` and `emulators`
  folders from your old S-Dock folder into the new one.
- Automatic updates aren't switched on yet: for a new beta, unzip it and copy your `data` and
  `emulators` folders across.
- The app isn't code-signed (see the SmartScreen note above).
